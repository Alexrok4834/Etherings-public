# Crypto World's Fair 2026

## In Rings We Trust

**Move. Play. Collect.**

EtheRings entered the competition with an existing Android MVP.

EtheRings is a mobile game where movement, gameplay, and collection are
connected through Rings. Move-to-Earn is one input into the larger game, not
the entire product. Movement creates resources, gameplay gives them purpose,
and Rings turn progression into a persistent collection.

## Pre-Existing Before The Official Hackathon Start

- native Android application;
- automatic step tracking;
- backend-authoritative Move-to-Earn;
- Draw / Raffle;
- off-chain ERT / ERU;
- Cooper Rings;
- inventory and progression;
- public Android testing.

## Not Implemented In The Pre-Hackathon Runtime

- Solana runtime integration;
- on-chain ERU;
- Smart Accounts;
- Silver NFT / Silver Ring Box on-chain flow;
- Cooper-to-Silver Box blockchain settlement;
- Silver progression on-chain;
- on-chain marketplace settlement.

## Current Hackathon Direction

Add the Solana blockchain layer during Alpha while retaining the existing game
as its foundation:

- ERT and Cooper remain authoritative off-chain;
- ERU, Silver Boxes, and Silver Rings move on-chain after their approved gates;
- users control assets through Smart Accounts and approved transactions can be
  sponsored;
- the Cooper-to-Silver Box flow validates reconciled off-chain ERT and on-chain
  ERU settlement;
- Silver progression and an internal fixed-price SOL marketplace complete the
  planned test vertical.

Planned Alpha work must not be interpreted as already implemented or deployed.
PostgreSQL and Solana are reconciled systems, not one atomic cross-system
transaction.
